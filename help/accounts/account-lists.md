# Account lists

By using account lists, you will be able to create filtered lists of accounts that can be used as journey audiences. 

Account lists are used to group and organize accounts based on specific criteria, such as industry, size, location, or any other relevant attributes. These lists are essential for targeting and personalizing marketing engagements, orchestrating customer journeys, and managing account-based marketing strategies in AJO B2B. 

Within AJO B2B, users can create, manage, and maintain account lists to support their marketing and sales initiatives. Account lists can be used to define target audiences, personalize customer experiences, and drive engagement with key accounts. 

Once account lists are created and populated with relevant accounts, they can be activated across various channels and touchpoints to deliver personalized and targeted marketing engagements. AJO B2B provides capabilities to activate account lists for account-based marketing campaigns, customer journeys, and other marketing activities. 

In summary, account lists in AJO B2B are a foundational element for managing and orchestrating account-based marketing and customer engagement activities. They enable users to organize, target, and personalize marketing engagements for key accounts, driving meaningful interactions and relationships.
